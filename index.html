For Indian salaried employees claiming House Rent Allowance, LTA, home-loan interest or other deductions with their employer. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, that statement is Form No. 124 (earlier Form 12BB). This kit is a plain checklist and rent log — not a tax calculator and not tax advice.

Not tax or legal advice. Rules and forms change. Always confirm on the official Income Tax India pages linked below and with your employer or a qualified tax professional before you submit or pay anything.

Why this exists

  • Income Tax India documents Form No. 124 as the employee statement of claims for TDS (replacing Form 12BB under the Income-tax Rules, 2026).
  • HRA particulars typically need landlord name, address, PAN when aggregate rent exceeds ₹1 lakh, relationship with the landlord if any, and annexures such as the rent agreement.
  • Most people scramble in January–March. A year-round checklist cuts missing receipts and last-minute WhatsApp chases.
