Frequently asked questions

What is Form No. 124?

Form No. 124 is the statement salaried employees give their employer listing claims such as HRA, LTA and home-loan interest so the employer can account for them when deducting TDS. Income Tax India lists it as replacing Form 12BB under the Income-tax Rules, 2026.

Is Form 12BB replaced by Form 124?

Yes. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 and the Income-tax Rules, 2026, Income Tax India documents Form No. 124 as the replacement for Form 12BB. Ask your employer which form their payroll uses for your tax year.

When is landlord PAN required for an HRA claim?

Landlord PAN is typically required when the aggregate rent you pay in the year exceeds ₹1 lakh. The free rent log on this page adds up your monthly rent so you can see whether you cross that line.

What documents do I need to claim HRA through my employer?

Typically the landlord's name and address, the landlord's PAN if yearly rent exceeds ₹1 lakh, your relationship with the landlord if any, and annexures such as the rent agreement and rent receipts. Your employer may ask for more, so check their payroll instructions.

When should I submit Form 124 to my employer?

Employers set their own deadline, and many collect proofs between January and March. Keeping receipts and the rent agreement ready through the year avoids the last-minute rush.

What if my landlord does not have a PAN?

Ask your employer what they accept in that case. The Pro pack includes a no-PAN declaration template you can adapt, but confirm the requirement with your employer or a qualified tax professional.
